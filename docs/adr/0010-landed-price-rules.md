# Landed price: one pure function, rule references in a table keyed by line code

Pricing stays one pure function, `landedPrice(input, env) → LandedResult`, with no I/O and no seam: there is one rule set, not several adapters. Every rate, fee and threshold it uses lives in `PRICING_RULES` (`packages/pricing/src/rules.ts`) next to its sources and an as-of date, and every breakdown line's code is a key of that table, so `PRICING_RULES[line.code]` is the line's rule reference. We chose this over a `rule` field on each line because the reference is the same for every Listing: carrying it per line would repeat constant data across every Listing in the Bundle (ADR 0004) and change the Bundle format for no new information. The web reads the table from the same `@cca/pricing` build that the Publisher used, so a reference describes the rules that produced the numbers; when a rule changes, the next publish recomputes every landed price.

Formula sources: `import-and-china.md` §4–§5 (DE→CZ, 2026-10-24) and `at-import-notes.md` (AT, 2026-10-25) in the research notes; the law and price lists are cited per entry in `rules.ts`.

## Rules

- **New means of transport** (ZDPH §19(1)(a), Directive 2006/112/EC Art. 2(2)(b), same test in Austria's UStG Binnenmarktregelung): new when ≤ 6 months since first registration OR ≤ 6,000 km; used only when both are exceeded. Registrations are known to the month, so the true age is taken as a range and a case the range cannot decide is priced as used and flagged `vatUncertain`. Absent registration counts as never registered only with Condition `new`.
- **New**: the goods are the price net of the Market's VAT (exempt intra-EU supply) plus seller extras normalized the same way, and the buyer pays 21% Czech VAT on both. A private seller's agreed price is taxed as is, not divided. A margin-scheme or unknown-basis price from a dealer is taken as is and flagged `approximate` (a new car cannot be sold under the margin scheme; an unknown basis may include VAT); a gross price from an unknown seller is assumed to include VAT, also flagged. A net price needs no flag: it is the base whoever sells.
- **Used**: the goods are what the buyer pays, the Market's VAT included (a private buyer cannot reclaim it); no Czech VAT. A net price from a VAT payer gets the Market's VAT added (CZ 21%, DE 19%, AT 20%). A seller's VAT status comes from the Seller: a private seller cannot charge VAT, so their "net" is taken as is; an unknown seller quoting net is assumed a VAT payer. Both cases are flagged `approximate`.
- **Czech Listings**: the asking price and seller extras as a private buyer pays them (the same net rule); no import fees and no transfer fee.
- **Austria**: as Germany with 20% USt, plus NoVA. Austrian consumer prices include NoVA (PrAG §9). A delivery to another EU state is NoVA-exempt (NoVAG §3(2)(1)), and NoVA paid on a used car is refunded to whoever exports it (NoVAG §12a). For exports from 2026-07-01 the car may be at most 48 months past its worldwide first registration when it is deregistered in Austria (BMF guidance on §12a from 2026-07-01). `LandedInput` has no deregistration date and a listed car may already be deregistered (first registered 2022-09, deregistered 2026-08 at 47 months, valued 2026-10 at 49 months: refundable), so the car's age at valuation cannot rule the refund out. The only rule a Listing can decide is BMF's cutoff: on exports from 2026-07-01 there is no refund for a car first registered worldwide before 2022-07-01; exports before 2026-07-01 fall under the old rule, which had no age limit. NoVA depends on CO₂, which `LandedInput` does not carry either, so it stays in the price (a new AT car's gross price is divided by 1.2 as if it were all VAT-inclusive) and the result is flagged `approximate` for every AT car except a used one valued from 2026-07-01 whose first registration is certainly before 2022-07, and a battery-electric one: zero-emission vehicles are NoVA-exempt (NoVAG §3(1)(1)), so their price holds no NoVA. Those are exact: any embedded NoVA is simply part of what the buyer pays.
- **Fees**: transport 12,000 CZK unless the user sets one (indicative); evidenční kontrola 790 CZK, plus 2,490 technical inspection and 950 emissions (none for a BEV) unless a valid foreign inspection covers the Czech interval. An expired inspection charges the full set at any age: cars used to carry passengers, such as taxis, are inspected yearly from the start (StVZO Anlage VIII 2.1, zákon 56/2001 Sb. §40). With validity unknown, a car certainly younger than 36 months needs only the identity check (no ordinary periodic inspection due yet in DE/AT/CZ), and an older one charges the full set and flags `inspectionAssumed`. Registration 800 CZK. Eco fee 3,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 CZK for Euro 2 / 1 / below; none for Euro 3+ or a BEV; unknown Euro class shows 0 and flags `ecoFeeUncertain`.
- **FX**: rates are per unit. The ČNB Source divides the fixing by its amount (HUF and others are quoted per 100); Pricing only multiplies.
- **Flags**: `approximate` and the other assumption flags are set only where the assumption can change the number; a contradiction that cannot (a private seller quoting net on a new car) is resolved silently.
- **Rounding**: each line is rounded to whole CZK, half up, after conversion; Czech VAT is computed on the unrounded base; `totalCzk` is the sum of the rounded lines, so the breakdown always adds up.

## Consequences

- A rule change is a data change in `rules.ts` plus a test; the `satisfies` clause makes a new line code without a rule entry a type error.
- Changing a rate or fee changes every landed price at the next publish; the sample Bundle must be regenerated when its numbers move.
- Removing the AT NoVA approximation needs CO₂ in `LandedInput`, and for used cars first registered from 2022-07 also the Austrian deregistration date (contract changes); the flag marks the affected Listings until then.
- China and other third countries are out of scope (user decision); `landedPrice` returns `unsupported-market` for any other Market.
